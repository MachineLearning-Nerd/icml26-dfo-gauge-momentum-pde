# Thank you

Thank you to Matteo Raviola and Benjamin Peherstorfer for making the
Dirac-Frenkel-Onsager paper and its source archive available. The paper gives
a clear account of gauge momentum for nonlinear PDE parametrizations and makes
the distinction between residual minimization and nullspace motion concrete.

This repository is an independent, deliberately bounded audit. It is not an
official implementation, author endorsement, or claim that the missing PDE
experiments have been reproduced.
